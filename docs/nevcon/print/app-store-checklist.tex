\section*{Common Rejection Reasons}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Issue} & \textbf{How to Avoid} \\
\midrule
Metadata & Screenshots must match actual app UI --- no mockups. \\
Privacy & Policy must be accessible \& accurate. Mention local-only storage. \\
Bugs & Test full onboarding flow. Crashes during review = instant rejection. \\
Permissions & Camera must show clear usage description. Already configured. \\
Login & No account required = good. Apple rejects forced sign-in. \\
Kids & Do NOT select ``Made for Kids'' --- triggers COPPA. App is for parents. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
