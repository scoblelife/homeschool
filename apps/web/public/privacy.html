Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 12, 2026

Homeschool ("the App") is developed by Scoble Life. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Summary

Homeschool is a local-first application. Your data is stored on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information.

Data Storage

All data you enter into the App — activities, student profiles, milestones, reading logs, events, and settings — is stored locally on your device using an on-device database. This data is not sent to any server operated by us.

Family Sync

If you choose to use the optional Family Sync feature, data is transmitted directly between your devices using peer-to-peer connections (WebRTC). A relay server may assist in establishing the connection, but your data is not stored on the relay server. Sync is entirely optional and initiated by you.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No personal information (names, emails, addresses)
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No location data
  • No crash reports sent to us
  • No cookies or web tracking

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. The App uses Expo for over-the-air updates, which may transmit your device platform and app version to Expo's servers. No personal data is included in these requests.

Children's Privacy

The App is designed for use by parents and guardians to manage homeschool records. Student information entered into the App stays on your device. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Camera and Photo Library

The App may request access to your device camera (for barcode scanning) and photo library. These permissions are used only for on-device functionality. No photos or camera data are transmitted off your device.

Data Deletion

Since your data is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting activities within the App or by uninstalling the App entirely.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@scoble.life.
