Log learning in seconds. Stay motivated all year.

Homeschool is the fast, private way to track your homeschool journey. Built by a homeschool parent, it makes daily logging effortless and turns your data into clear weekly insights.

QUICK LOGGING
- Log activities in under 10 seconds with natural text input
- Type "Math worksheet 30 min" and the app extracts the details
- One-tap templates for frequently logged activities
- Six activity types: worksheet, video, reading, writing, hands-on, interactive
- Support for multiple students with individual profiles

TRACK PROGRESS
- Weekly summary: activities completed, hours logged, subjects covered
- Daily activity history with subject breakdowns
- Reports by date range (7, 30, or 90 days)
- See which subjects get the most time and where gaps might be

MILESTONES & GOALS
- Set learning milestones with target dates
- Track status from not started through completed
- Earn stars for completed milestones
- Weekly wins celebrate your consistency

READING & LIBRARY
- Track books by title, author, genre, and reading level
- Log reading sessions with page progress
- See each student's reading status at a glance

EVENTS & FIELD TRIPS
- Plan educational outings, co-op meetings, and social events
- Track dates, locations, costs, and learning outcomes
- Associate events with subjects and students

CALENDAR & PLANNER
- Monthly calendar view with activity and event markers
- Weekly planner with milestone checklists
- Today dashboard shows your daily overview and weekly stats

FAMILY SYNC
- Peer-to-peer sync between family devices
- No cloud account required
- Works offline, syncs when connected
- Scan a QR code to link devices

PRIVACY FIRST
- Local-first: your data stays on your devices
- No ads, no tracking, no data collection
- No account or login required
- Works completely offline

Whether you follow a structured curriculum or take a flexible approach, Homeschool keeps your records organized without getting in your way.
